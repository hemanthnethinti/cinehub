# 02 - Product Definition

## Problem Statement
Film professionals lack a dedicated platform that combines the professional networking of LinkedIn with the visual portfolio capabilities of Instagram or Vimeo.

## Target Users & Personas
- **The Creator**: Directors, cinematographers, writers looking to showcase portfolios and find crew members.
- **The Talent**: Actors, models, voice artists looking for auditions and networking.
- **The Cinephile**: Fans and reviewers looking to follow creators and discover new independent projects.

## Use Cases
- A director discovering a cinematographer in their city based on uploaded showreels.
- A user updating their skills and location to appear in local production searches.
- Fans following their favorite indie directors for updates.

## Current Modules
1. **Authentication**: Secure JWT-based login, registration, and password recovery.
2. **Profile**: Rich user profiles, avatars, skill tracking, and follower/following social graph.

## Future Modules
- **Projects**: Portfolio items linking multiple users (e.g., "Director", "Actor") to a single video/film entity.
- **Messaging**: Real-time Socket.IO chat between users.
- **Feed/Discover**: Algorithm-driven feed of project updates and new creators.
